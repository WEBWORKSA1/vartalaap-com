FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Vartalaap free?

Yes. The phrasebook, Conversation Lab, fluency tools, guides, level test and monthly Speak-Off are free. Optional one-to-one coaching is paid and arranged with independent coaches.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Speech features work best in Chrome, Edge or Safari; on other browsers you can type your answers.

Is my voice recorded or stored?

No. Your browser converts speech to text for scoring. We do not upload or keep audio.

Is this an official English or IELTS test?

No. Our level test and band estimator are practice guides. Only official test bodies issue certified scores.

I'm a coach or institute. Can I join?

Yes — see Careers & Talent to teach with us, or Advertise & Partner to receive matched student enquiries.
