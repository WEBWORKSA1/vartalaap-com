Watch, then speak

Ten talks worth your time — each with one practice task. Watching alone won't make you fluent; doing the task within an hour will.

How to speak so that people want to listen

Julian Treasure · TED

Task: Read one paragraph aloud three times — first flat, then varying pitch, then with deliberate pauses. Record all three.

How to speak

Patrick Winston · MIT OpenCourseWare

Task: Plan a 2-minute talk with an “empowerment promise” in the first sentence.

Think fast, talk smart

Stanford Graduate School of Business

Task: Do three Just-a-minute topics using the “what – so what – now what” structure.

The secret to great public speaking

Chris Anderson · TED

Task: Write your one big idea in 15 words, then explain it to a friend without notes.

The secrets of learning a new language

Lýdia Machóva · TED

Task: Pick one method you enjoy and schedule 15 minutes of it daily for a week.

How to learn any language in six months

Chris Lonsdale · TEDx

Task: Learn the top 20 phrases in one phrasebook category this week.

The best way to get fluent English

English is EZ with Connor

Task: Run one Conversation Lab scene twice, a day apart, and compare scores.

Your body language may shape who you are

Amy Cuddy · TED

Task: Before your next interview or call, stand tall for two minutes, then speak slower than feels natural.

The power of introverts

Susan Cain · TED

Task: Prepare two questions before your next meeting so you can contribute without improvising.

How great leaders inspire action

Simon Sinek · TED

Task: Rewrite your “Tell me about yourself” to start with why. Try it in the builder.
