Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Essential browser storage (ours)

We use your browser's local storage — not tracking cookies — to remember your theme, cookie choice, saved phrases, practice scores, streak and level-test result. This data never leaves your device unless you submit a form.

Advertising cookies (Google)

Google AdSense sets cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. Manage them at Google Ads Settings.

Embedded services

YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, after you click play) and Google Fonts may set or read cookies under their own policies.

Your choices

Use the cookie banner, your browser settings, or clear site data at any time. Blocking cookies won't stop the practice tools from working.
