About

वार्तालाप means conversation

We started Vartalaap with one observation: most people don't have a knowledge problem. They have a practice problem.

Across India, students study English for over a decade and still freeze when an interviewer says, “Tell me about yourself.” Abroad, the children and partners of Hindi speakers understand family conversations but can't join them. In both cases the missing piece is the same: a safe place to speak out loud, often, and get feedback.

Vartalaap is that place. Everything here is designed around speaking rather than reading about speaking:

  • A phrasebook of real, everyday sentences in English and Hindi, with audio.
  • A Conversation Lab where you speak your side of a dialogue and get scored instantly.
  • Fluency tools that measure pace, fillers and common slips.
  • Interview and GD prep built around how hiring actually works.
  • A monthly Speak-Off that gives you a reason to record yourself — and a shot at prizes.

What we believe

  • Indian English is valid English. We help you adapt to your audience, not erase your identity.
  • Short and daily beats long and rare. Ten minutes a day changes more than a weekend workshop.
  • Privacy by default. Speech is processed in your browser; we don't store your voice.
  • Free core, optional coaching. The tools stay free. Learners who want a human coach can book a free demo with an independent, vetted teacher.

How Vartalaap is funded

We earn from clearly labelled advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, partnerships with coaching providers and voluntary supporter contributions. None of these change what we teach or how we score you.
