Hindi phrases for travel in India

Hindi · 8 min read · Updated October 2026

English goes a long way in Indian cities, but a few Hindi phrases change how people respond to you — warmer smiles, fairer prices and real help when you need it.

Hindi is widely spoken across North and Central India. In the South, East and North-East, regional languages dominate, but basic Hindi is still often understood in cities and tourist areas.

Shopping and bargaining

Bargaining is normal in street markets but not in malls or fixed-price shops. Be friendly, smile, and be ready to walk away.

India's single emergency number is 112.

Etiquette that matters as much as words

  • Greet with “Namaste” and folded hands, especially with elders.
  • Address drivers and shopkeepers as bhaiya (brother) or didi (sister) — friendly and respectful.
  • Use your right hand to give and receive money or food.
  • Remove shoes before entering homes and temples.

Practise these out loud in the auto-rickshaw and market scenes before you go.
