How to learn a phrase so it sticks

  1. Listen twice without reading. Notice where the stress falls.
  2. Shadow it — speak along with the audio, matching rhythm, not just words.
  3. Say it to someone within 24 hours, even a friend on WhatsApp voice notes.
  4. Review on day 1, 3, 7 and 21. Spaced repetition beats cramming.

Reading Hindi romanisation

We use a simple, readable style rather than a strict academic system:

  • aa = long “a” as in father (आ)
  • ee = long “i” as in see (ई)
  • oo = long “u” as in food (ऊ)
  • kh, gh, chh, th, dh, ph = breathy versions of k, g, ch, t, d, p
  • n after a vowel at the end (hain, nahin) is nasal — say it through the nose.
